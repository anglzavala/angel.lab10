Windows

Best for compatibility and gaming

Windows is the most familiar choice for everyday users, students, and gamers due to its wide software support.

  • Strong support for PC gaming and mainstream apps
  • Excellent for business and office work
  • Wide hardware availability and easy upgrades
🍎

macOS

Best for design and Apple ecosystem

macOS offers smooth hardware integration, strong performance, and a polished experience for creators and professionals.

  • Strong performance with Apple silicon
  • Excellent for creative workflows and productivity
  • Limited to Apple hardware
🐧

Linux

Best for customization and servers

Linux is lightweight, flexible, and open-source. It powers developers, servers, and advanced users who want control.

  • High customization and security options
  • Excellent for programming and server environments
  • Steeper learning curve for beginners
